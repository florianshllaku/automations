What Each Stage Does
The Pipeline, Unwrapped
Each box on the diagram — in one sentence.
1
Trigger
Telegram Bot
Sending /news in Telegram fires the entire pipeline. No dashboards, no logins.
2
Scraper
Playwright
Visits the news source, pulls fresh health articles, and skips anything already processed.
3
Human Check #1
Telegram Approval
Each article shows up in Telegram with ✅ Create / ❌ Skip buttons. Only approved ones move forward.
4
Script Writer
OpenAI GPT
Rewrites the article into a punchy 110–130 word Albanian voiceover — tuned for short-form attention.
5
Scene Planner
OpenAI GPT
Breaks the script into 5–8 timed scenes and writes a detailed image prompt for each one.
6
Image Generation
fal.ai
Renders one cinematic 4K portrait image per scene. Brand-character scenes use a reference photo for identity.
7
Voice + Subtitles
OpenAI TTS · Gladia
Speaks the script in natural Albanian, then transcribes it back to word-level timestamps for clean subtitles.
8
Final Render
ffmpeg
Animates each image with Ken Burns motion, syncs audio, burns in subtitles, drops the logo. Outputs 1080×1920 MP4.
9
Human Check #2
Telegram Approval
A "VIDEO READY" notification with a preview link. One tap to publish — or one tap to skip.
10
Publish
Google Drive · Buffer
Uploads to Drive, hands the link to Buffer, schedules to TikTok at the optimal time. Done.

Run Sequence
From /news to TikTok in ~5 Minutes
T + 0s
Command fires
User sends /news in Telegram. The bot wakes the pipeline and starts scraping.
T + 30s
Article shortlist arrives
Fresh, never-seen articles arrive in Telegram with ✅ / ❌ buttons. Human picks one.
T + 1 min
GPT writes the script
Rewritten into a viral-style 40-second Albanian voiceover, plus an 8-scene visual plan as JSON.
T + 2–3 min
fal.ai renders the visuals
5–8 cinematic 4K portrait images, one per scene, with consistent brand-character placement where required.
T + 3 min
Voice + subtitles built
TTS speaks the script, Gladia gives word-level timestamps, GPT corrects transcription drift.
T + 4 min
ffmpeg ships the MP4
Ken Burns animation, audio sync, burned subtitles, branded logo — packed into 1080×1920 H.264.
T + ~5 min
Live on TikTok
Uploaded to Drive, scheduled by Buffer, posted to TikTok at optimal time. Confirmation pings Telegram.
