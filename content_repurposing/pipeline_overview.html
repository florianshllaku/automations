Full Pipeline — Step by Step

Every action from the first trigger to the published TikTok

Phase 1 🔍
Discovery
Trigger the pipeline and choose which articles to produce
01
📲
Telegram Command — Trigger
User sends the /news command to the Telegram bot. This fires up the full pipeline and begins article discovery.
Telegram Bot
02
🕷️
Web Scraping — Article Discovery
A Playwright headless browser visits Telegrafi.com (health & nutrition section), extracts titles, URLs, and dates across up to 2 pages. Already-processed articles are filtered out using a local seen_articles.json checkpoint file.
Playwright Telegrafi.com
✋
👤
Human Checkpoint #1
Article Approval via Telegram
Each new article is sent to Telegram with inline buttons [✅ Create] and [❌ Skip]. The human reviews the headline and decides. Approved articles move to Phase 2. Skipped ones are archived and never processed.
👤 Human Telegram
↓   Approved article passed to AI
Phase 2 🤖
AI Content Generation
AI writes the script, plans scenes, and generates all visual imagery
04
📄
Fetch Full Article Body
Playwright loads the approved article page and extracts the complete body text. This is the raw source material that all AI steps work from.
Playwright
05
✍️
AI Script Generation
ChatGPT rewrites the article into a punchy 110–130 word Albanian voiceover script. Style: catchy, viral, conversational — designed to hold attention for ~40 seconds. Saved as .txt.
OpenAI GPT Albanian · 40s
06
🎨
AI Visual Scene Planning
ChatGPT reads the script and outputs structured JSON: 5–8 scenes, each with a duration (4–7 sec), a detailed image generation prompt, and whether to feature the brand character. This JSON drives the next step.
OpenAI GPT JSON · 5–8 scenes
07
🖼️
AI Image Generation — One Per Scene
fal.ai generates a 4K portrait image for each scene using the detailed prompts. Scenes flagged for the brand character use a reference photo to keep visual identity consistent. Output: 5–8 JPEG images at 9:16 aspect ratio.
fal.ai 4K · 9:16
↓   Script + Images passed to production
Phase 3 🎬
Media Production
Voice synthesis, subtitle creation, and full video assembly with animation effects
08
🎙️
Text-to-Speech Audio
OpenAI TTS converts the script into natural Albanian speech using the echo voice at 1.2× speed — tuned for short-form pacing. Output: .mp3 audio file.
OpenAI TTS MP3 · 1.2×
09
💬
Subtitle Generation & AI Correction
The audio is uploaded to Gladia API which returns word-level timestamps. These are grouped into ~11-word chunks and formatted as two-line SRT entries. A final GPT pass corrects any transcription errors against the original script.
Gladia GPT Correction
10
🎞️
Video Assembly — ffmpeg
ffmpeg animates each image with a random Ken Burns effect (zoom, pan, tilt). Clips are concatenated, synced with the audio track, subtitles are burned in (yellow, bold, Inter), and the brand logo is overlaid top-right. Output: 1080×1920 H.264 MP4.
ffmpeg 1080×1920 MP4
↓   Finished video passed to publishing
Phase 4 📱
Publishing
Cloud upload, human review of finished video, then automated TikTok scheduling
11
☁️
Upload to Google Drive
The finished MP4 is uploaded to a shared Google Drive folder via OAuth. A public direct-download link is generated — this URL is what gets passed to Buffer for TikTok posting.
Google Drive
✋
👤
Human Checkpoint #2
Video Approval via Telegram
A "VIDEO READY!" notification is sent with a Google Drive preview link and buttons [✅ Post to TikTok] and [❌ Skip]. The human watches the video and decides. Rejected videos are archived; approved ones go live.
👤 Human Telegram
13
🎵
Post to TikTok — Buffer API
Buffer's GraphQL API receives the Google Drive URL and the video title. It queues the post for the TikTok channel and schedules it automatically for optimal posting time. A confirmation with the scheduled time is sent back to Telegram.
Buffer API TikTok
🎵
TikTok Video — Live
A fully produced, subtitled, branded short-form video is scheduled and published to TikTok. The original article has been transformed into engaging vertical content — without any manual editing, recording, or design work.
1080 × 1920 px H.264 · AAC Albanian subtitles Ken Burns animated Brand watermark Auto-scheduled
